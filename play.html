Ridge run

Up the trail on the ridges of the channel banner. Jump the rocks and pines; the choughs fly low or high, so go over the low ones and stay down for the high ones.

Space / ↑ / tap: jump, hold for higher · ↓: drop faster · Esc: pause. Your best distance stays in this browser.
