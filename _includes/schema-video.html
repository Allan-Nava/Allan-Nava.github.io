{%- comment -%} VideoObject JSON-LD for a post that embeds a YouTube video (page.video_id, set by _plugins/seo.rb): what Google needs to show the post in its video results. The values are jsonify'd, then every ":" becomes "\u003a" — valid JSON, and jemoji never sees a `:shortcode:` in the script (CLAUDE.md, "jemoji entra anche negli script"). The post's own BlogPosting stays as jekyll-seo-tag writes it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.video_id -%} {%- assign video_name = page.title | strip_emoji_shortcodes -%} {%- assign video_thumb = page.thumb | default: page.image | default: "https://i.ytimg.com/vi/" | append: page.video_id | append: "/hqdefault.jpg" -%} {%- assign video_desc = page.description | default: site.description | strip_html -%} {%- endif -%}
